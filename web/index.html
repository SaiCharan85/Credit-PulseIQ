Eval-first credit intelligence

Every number traced back to the filing it came from.

CreditPulse IQ reads a company's SEC filings and reports how much financial distress the evidence shows — with each figure recomputed from its source and each claim dated. Nothing after the prediction date is visible to the analysis, so what you read is what a reader could have known at the time, not hindsight dressed as foresight.

How an assessment is produced

1

Filter to the date

Every fact filed after the prediction date is withheld — about half of a company's history. The date is not a setting the model can change.

2

Investigate

The agent picks one tool, reads the result, and chooses the next call from what it found. It also reads what filings say: going-concern language, covenant breaches, delisting notices.

3

Verify, then publish

A deterministic critic recomputes every cited figure against source. Anything it cannot reproduce blocks the memo outright rather than being flagged.

What it does not do

  • No decisions. It states risk. It never recommends lending, investing or pricing.
  • Absolute probabilities do not transfer. The test universe is enriched with distressed filers far above the population base rate. Ranking transfers; calibrated probabilities do not.
  • Earnings quality is demo-grade. Four approaches measured 0.51–0.61 AUC with a clean leak canary, so those observations are context only and never move the graded signal.
  • US SEC filers only, and one model over one time window.
  • It has never chosen to abstain. Every abstention in 200 cases was a protocol failure, so "it will tell you when it doesn't know" is unexercised.
What this isAn evidence service for credit analysis. It reports what filings say and what the numbers are; a qualified person decides what to do.
